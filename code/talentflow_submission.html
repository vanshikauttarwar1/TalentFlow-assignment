Following a comprehensive audit of our ATS data architecture, we have aligned our upcoming engineering investments with validated data. This memo outlines our findings and the resulting 6 engineer-week roadmap.

What we found

  • Referrals are the largest attributed source for hires: While current reporting suggests Job Boards are tied for #1 (26.9%), this is due to an inaccurate attribution model that systematically undercounts referrals. When using application-level data, Referrals are our largest attributed source for hires (38.5%), exceeding Job Boards (23.1%).
  • Offer acceptance is higher than the reported figure: The reported 72.2% figure includes 5 pending offers in the denominator. When isolated strictly to decided offers, our resolved-offer acceptance rate is 83.9%.
  • Job capacity tracking is out of sync: We found an 11 out of 24 (45.8%) mismatch rate between job opening status, headcount, and actual hires, including open requisitions that have already met headcount.
  • Candidate emails are 100% unique: We found only 3 duplicate application pairs (resulting in just 1 invalid double-count out of 350 applications).

What we’re doing (6 Engineer-Weeks)

We are prioritizing targeted fixes that correct our reporting and reduce manual tracking errors.

  1. Application-Level Source Tracking (3 weeks): We will enforce a Source field at the Application level (prioritizing Referred By data).
  2. Automate Job Opening Status (2 weeks): We will build logic to automatically set a Job Opening to "Filled" when hires equal headcount.
  3. Fix Offer Acceptance Dashboard (1 week): We will update the reporting logic to accurately calculate Accepted / (Accepted + Declined).

What we’re not doing

  • Job-Board Integrations: We will not allocate resources to Job-Board integration while application-level attribution shows Job Boards at 23.1% of hires.
  • Offer Acceptance Optimization: We will not invest engineering weeks into candidate-closing tools based on the current acceptance-rate data.
  • Automated Application Deduplication Engine: Frequency of application duplicates (1.7%) is too low to justify the complexity.

6-Week Engineering Roadmap

Justifying data: 24 of the 27 referred applications are currently misattributed. True application-level attribution reveals Referrals drive 38.5% of hires.

3. Fix Offer Acceptance Dashboard Metric

What we build: Update reporting logic to calculate Accepted / (Accepted + Declined) and add a UI flag for "Pending" offers containing ambiguous past decision dates.

Justifying data: True acceptance rate is 83.9% (26/31 decided offers).

Not To Build

  • Job-Board Integrations: Application-level attribution shows Job Boards at 23.1% of hires, behind Referrals.
  • Offer Acceptance Optimization: Candidate-facing closing tools have minimal ROI when 83.9% of decided offers are already accepted.
  • Automated Application Deduplication Engine: The issue is strictly isolated to duplicate applications for the same job (3 pairs total). The frequency is too low to justify complex algorithmic merging.

Offer Acceptance Rate (OAR) Spec

Goal: Accurately measure the percentage of decided offers that candidates accept, while maintaining clear visibility into the pipeline of pending offers and flagging data hygiene issues.

Target Metric: 83.9%

Definition Logic

  • Primary Metric (OAR): Accepted / (Accepted + Declined)
  • Numerator: Count of unique Offer ID records where Status = "Accepted"
  • Denominator: Count of unique Offer ID records where Status IN ("Accepted", "Declined")

Secondary Metric: Pending Offer Pipeline

Calculated as: Pending / Total Offers Sent (currently 5/36 = 13.9%). Pending offers must be completely excluded from the primary OAR calculation until a final decision is reached.

Edge Cases & Handling

  • Data Hygiene Flag (Conflicting Pending Dates): 4 "Pending" offers have a date populated in the Decision On field. These should be flagged for recruiter review, not automatically set to Declined.
  • Withdrawn/Rescinded Offers: If an offer is rescinded (status = "Withdrawn"), exclude it from the denominator entirely.
  • Duplicate Offers / Re-hires: Count by unique Offer ID, not Candidate ID.
  • Dashboard Display: If Denominator = 0, the dashboard must display "N/A - No decided offers" to prevent divide-by-zero errors.

Data Quality & Trust Audit

Offers Data Verification

  • Consistency Issue: 4 out of 36 offers (11.1%) have a direct contradiction between the Status and Decision On fields (Pending status but past decision dates populated).
  • Impact: Requires a data-quality warning flag on the dashboard, but does not change the strictly defined Acceptance Rate (83.9%).

Job Openings Data Verification

  • Consistency Issue: 11 out of 24 openings (45.8%) have a direct contradiction between their Status, Headcount, and the actual number of Hired applications (e.g., 4 Open with headcount met).
  • Impact: Supports the roadmap to improve job status synchronization and capacity tracking.

Applications Data Verification

  • Consistency Issue: 3 duplicate application pairs exist (where the exact same Candidate applied to the exact same Job Opening).
  • Impact: Because the frequency is extremely low (1.7%), this supports our decision NOT to build an algorithmic deduplication engine.
  • Trustworthy Elements: 100% of applications correctly link to a valid Candidate and Job Opening.

Candidates Data Verification

  • Consistency Issue: 24 applications (88.9%) belong to Candidates whose static Source is NOT listed as Referral, despite having a Referred By value.
  • Impact: Provides strong evidence for the roadmap to build application-level source tracking. Correcting this shows Referrals as the largest attributed source for hires (38.5%).
